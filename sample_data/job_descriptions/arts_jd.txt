Job Title: Creative Art Director & Visual Artist
Department: Creative & Visual Arts
Location: Studio / Hybrid
Required Experience: 3 to 5 years
Minimum Education: Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) or Visual Arts

Job Overview:
Looking for an imaginative Art Director to lead visual art production, conceptual design, illustration, and exhibition curation across high-impact visual campaigns.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Visual Artistry: Conceptualizing fine arts, digital illustration, storyboarding, and concept art.
- Art Direction: Guiding creative direction, layout composition, typography, and color theory.
- Project Curation: Exhibition design, gallery curation, and brand visual storytelling.
- Digital Tools: Advanced Adobe Creative Suite, digital drawing tablets, and 3D visualization.
- Collaboration: Leading cross-functional teams, client pitching, and creative reviews.
