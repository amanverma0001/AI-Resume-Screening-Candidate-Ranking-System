Job Title: Fitness Coach & Personal Trainer
Department: Health & Wellness
Location: Fitness Center / Wellness Club
Required Experience: 2 to 5 years
Minimum Education: Certified Fitness Trainer (ACE / ACSM / NSCA / ISSA) or B.P.Ed

Job Overview:
We are hiring a passionate Fitness Coach to provide client personal training, functional workout programming, and holistic nutrition planning.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Personal Training: Designing tailored strength training, HIIT, and weight loss workout regimens.
- Health Assessment: Body composition analysis, posture evaluation, and movement screening.
- Nutrition Planning: Advising on caloric balance, macronutrients, and dietary lifestyle adjustments.
- Safety & Physiology: Adhering to exercise physiology principles, injury prevention, and first aid / CPR certification.
