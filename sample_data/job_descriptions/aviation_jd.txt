Job Title: Aviation Maintenance Engineer (AME)
Department: Aircraft Engineering & Operations
Location: Airport Hangar
Required Experience: 3 to 6 years
Minimum Education: AME License / B.Tech in Aeronautical or Aerospace Engineering

Job Overview:
Seeking a certified Aviation Maintenance Engineer to perform scheduled and unscheduled aircraft maintenance, avionics testing, and flight safety inspections.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Aircraft Maintenance: Airframe inspections, propulsion systems repair, jet engine overhaul, and hydraulics testing.
- Avionics & Electronics: Diagnostic testing of aircraft electrical systems, radar, and navigation avionics.
- Regulatory Compliance: Adherence to DGCA and FAA airworthiness directives, maintenance logs, and flight safety protocols.
- Quality Assurance: Component life-cycle tracking and rigorous safety audits.
