Job Title: Senior Accountant & Financial Auditor
Department: Finance & Accounts
Location: On-site / Hybrid
Required Experience: 3 to 5 years
Minimum Education: B.Com, M.Com, or CA Inter / MBA Finance

Job Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Accountant to oversee financial record-keeping, balance sheet preparation, and taxation compliance. The ideal candidate will manage ledgers, execute reconciliations, and ensure adherence to GST and income tax statutory regulations.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Financial Accounting: General Ledger maintenance, Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), and bank reconciliation.
- Taxation & Compliance: Preparation and filing of GST, TDS, and corporate Income Tax returns.
- Tools & Software: Proficiency in Tally ERP, QuickBooks, Microsoft Excel, and SAP FICO.
- Auditing & Reporting: Preparation of quarterly balance sheets, trial balance, and P&L financial reporting.
- Professional Competencies: Strong budgeting, cost accounting, problem solving, and attention to detail.
