Job Title: Senior UI/UX & Graphic Designer
Department: Product Design
Location: Hybrid
Required Experience: 3 to 5 years
Minimum Education: Bachelor in Design (B.Des), Human-Computer Interaction, or Graphic Arts

Job Overview:
Seeking a creative UI/UX Designer to craft intuitive web and mobile application experiences, design system components, and marketing graphics.
