Job Title: Corporate Legal Counsel & Advocate
Department: Legal Affairs
Location: On-site
Required Experience: 3 to 6 years
Minimum Education: LL.B or LL.M (Registered with Bar Council)

Job Overview:
Looking for a diligent Legal Counsel / Advocate to manage corporate contract drafting, litigation management, regulatory compliance, and dispute resolution.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Legal Drafting: Drafting and reviewing commercial contracts, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), master service agreements, and vendor contracts.
- Litigation & Court Advocacy: Representing the organization in court appearances, preparing briefs, pleadings, and arbitration petitions.
- Legal Research: Conducting comprehensive legal research, case law analysis, and statutory due diligence.
- Regulatory Compliance: Ensuring adherence to corporate law, labor law, and intellectual property (IP) regulations.
- Soft Skills: Persuasive negotiation, client advocacy, and critical thinking.
