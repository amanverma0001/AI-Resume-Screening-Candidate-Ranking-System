Job Title: Civil & Construction Site Engineer
Department: Project Construction & Infrastructure
Location: Construction Site
Required Experience: 3 to 6 years
Minimum Education: B.Tech or B.E in Civil Engineering

Job Overview:
Seeking a qualified Civil Site Engineer to supervise structural construction, concrete works, contractor management, and site safety compliance.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Site Supervision: Managing day-to-day site execution, civil engineering construction, and contractor coordination.
- Structural & Design: Interpreting architectural blueprints, structural analysis, and AutoCAD drawings.
- Quantity & Estimation: Material estimation, bar bending schedules (BBS), and bill verification.
- Quality & Safety: Enforcing site safety standards, concrete technology quality testing, and building codes.
