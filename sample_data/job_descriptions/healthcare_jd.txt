Job Title: Clinical Nurse & Patient Care Specialist
Department: Patient Care & Healthcare
Location: Multi-Specialty Hospital
Required Experience: 2 to 5 years
Minimum Education: B.Sc Nursing or GNM (Registered Nurse)

Job Overview:
Seeking a compassionate Clinical Nurse to deliver professional patient care, monitor vital signs, administer medications, and assist in clinical diagnosis.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Direct Patient Care: Monitoring vital signs, medication administration, intravenous (IV) therapy, and wound dressing.
- Emergency & ICU Care: Basic Life Support (BLS), CPR certification, emergency care, and triage support.
- Clinical Documentation: Maintaining accurate electronic health records (EHR) and nursing care plans.
- Infection Control: Enforcing hospital hygiene, infection control protocols, and patient safety guidelines.
