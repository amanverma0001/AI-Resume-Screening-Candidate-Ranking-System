Job Title: Senior Sales Executive & Account Manager
Department: Commercial Sales
Location: Field / Office
Required Experience: 2 to 5 years
Minimum Education: Bachelor in Business, Commerce, or Marketing

Job Overview:
Seeking a target-driven Sales Executive to manage high-volume customer accounts, generate qualified leads, conduct sales presentations, and achieve monthly revenue quotas.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Sales Execution: Prospecting new clients, cold calling, conducting product demos, and closing deals.
- Account Management: Nurturing existing customer accounts, upselling, and boosting customer retention.
- CRM & Pipeline: Managing leads, deals, and sales pipeline in CRM software (Salesforce, HubSpot).
- Negotiation: Commercial pricing negotiation, objection handling, and relationship building.
