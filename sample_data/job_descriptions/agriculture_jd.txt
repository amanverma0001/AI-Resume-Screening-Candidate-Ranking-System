Job Title: Agriculture Specialist & Agronomist
Department: Agronomy & Farm Operations
Location: Field / On-site
Required Experience: 2 to 5 years
Minimum Education: B.Sc or M.Sc in Agriculture / Agronomy

Job Overview:
Seeking a dedicated Agronomist to optimize crop management, soil health, irrigation schedules, and sustainable farming methodologies across agricultural estates.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Crop & Soil Management: Conducting soil science assessments, fertilizer management, crop rotation, and harvesting planning.
- Pest & Disease Control: Implementing integrated pest control and plant disease diagnostics.
- Irrigation & Sustainability: Managing modern drip and sprinkler irrigation systems, water conservation, and sustainable agriculture practices.
- Research & Field Trials: Designing agricultural research trials, analyzing seed yield data, and plant breeding observations.
- Communication: Training rural field staff and delivering farm operations reports.
