Job Title: Human Resources (HR) & Talent Acquisition Specialist
Department: Human Resources
Location: Corporate Office / Hybrid
Required Experience: 2 to 5 years
Minimum Education: MBA in HR, Master in Human Resources, or BBA

Job Overview:
We are seeking an HR Specialist to manage end-to-end recruitment, employee engagement, HR policy implementation, and onboarding workflows.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Talent Acquisition: Sourcing candidate profiles, screening interviews, campus drives, and applicant tracking.
- HR Operations: Formulating HR policies, employee onboarding, attendance tracking, and exit management.
- Employee Relations: Driving employee engagement, conflict resolution, and grievance handling.
- Compliance: Ensuring adherence to labor laws, PF, ESI, and POSH compliance.
- Soft Skills: Active listening, empathetic communication, and organizational leadership.
