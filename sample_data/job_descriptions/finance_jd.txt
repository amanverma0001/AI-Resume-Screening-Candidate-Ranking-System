Job Title: Corporate Finance & Financial Planning Analyst
Department: Corporate Finance
Location: Corporate Office
Required Experience: 3 to 5 years
Minimum Education: MBA in Finance, CFA, or B.Com Honors

Job Overview:
Looking for a Financial Analyst to build financial models, analyze capital expenditure, manage budgeting forecasts, and deliver strategic financial reports.

Key Responsibilities & Required Skills:
- Financial Modeling: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), 3-statement models, scenario modeling in Microsoft Excel.
- Planning & Forecasting: Annual budgeting, quarterly variance analysis, and cash flow forecasting.
- Corporate Finance: Capital structure evaluation, investment appraisal, and financial reporting.
- Compliance: Understanding of GAAP, IFRS, and corporate taxation.
